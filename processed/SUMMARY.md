## Processing decisions

- **Features**: 42,536 / 60,675 genes kept (detected with TPM > 0 in ≥ 20% of the
  128 PNAS samples). Label-blind, so safe to apply before CV.
- **Transform**: `log2(TPM + 1)`.
- **Deferred to the modeling CV loop** (must be fit on training folds only, to
  avoid leakage): z-score standardization, variance/supervised feature selection.
- **CV**: 3-fold, **grouped by donor (`poiseid`) and stratified by label at the
  donor level**. Recurrence is a donor-level property, so splitting at the donor
  level keeps every donor wholly in one fold (no repeated-measures leakage) and
  spreads the 10 positive donors evenly (3 / 4 / 3 per fold).
- **Validation cohort** is held out entirely as an external test set — never used
  in CV. It shares 100% of the retained feature genes.

## Class balance

| Task | Unit | Positives | Negatives | CV folds (test) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cancer vs normal | 128 samples / 76 groups | 96 cancer | 32 normal | label split ~32/30/34 |
| Recurrence | 96 samples / 44 donors | 10 pos donors (28 samples) | 34 / 68 | pos donors 3/4/3 |
| Validation – cancer | 161 samples | 83 BC | 78 normal | external |
| Validation – recurrence | 83 BC samples | 8 recurrent | 75 | external |

## Caveats for modeling

- Recurrence effective n is **44 donors / 10 positives** — expect poor, unstable
  performance (this is the stated expectation). Report donor-level metrics.
- Severe class imbalance in validation recurrence (8 vs 75).
- Likely batch effects between PNAS and validation cohorts; transfer is optional.
